# ஆத்திப்பூ — வாளை நிறுத்திய தமிழ்
## HISTORICAL ARCHITECTURAL & ENVIRONMENT RESEARCH BIBLE
### Research Gate for Sangam Era Uraiyur & Kaveri Basin Urban Environment

This document establishes the historical, archaeological, and literary research foundation for all architectural environments, civic spaces, domestic quarters, markets, and sacred shrines in the 3D animated film **“ஆத்திப்பூ — வாளை நிறுத்திய தமிழ்” (Aathipoo — The Tamil That Stayed the Sword)**.

---

### METHODOLOGY & EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

Every visual and spatial element in the film is categorized into one of three methodological tiers:

- **Tier A (Directly Supported Detail)**: Verified by primary Sangam literary texts (principally *Purananuru 45*, with supporting references from *Pattinappalai*, *Maduraikkanji*, and *Perumpanarruppadai* cross-referenced) or archaeological excavation reports (e.g., Uraiyur excavations by the Archaeological Survey of India / University of Madras, Arikamedu, Kodumanal, Kaveripumpattinam).
- **Tier B (Plausible Artistic Reconstruction)**: Architecturally and materially coherent with Early Historic Tamil Nadu (3rd c. BCE – 3rd c. CE) construction techniques, geography, climate, and craft technologies, without claiming to be an exact portrait of a specific vanished building.
- **Tier C (Uncertain Detail Deliberately Omitted)**: Post-Sangam, medieval, or modern elements explicitly excluded to prevent anachronism (e.g., medieval Chola tall granite gopurams, massive stone pillared mandapas of the 10th–12th centuries, later Chola bronze iconography, modern New World crops, or electricity/signage).

### Sightlines & Walking Routes
1. **From Courtyard to Fort Gate**: The potter's home lies near the Kaveri riverfront clay beds. Walking southward down the residential lane passes the civic tree *manram* and market street before reaching the fortress walls.
2. **From Fort Gate to Citadel Hall**: Passing through the fortified inner gate corridor brings the visitor to the audience pavilion of Nedunkilli.
3. **From Citadel to Outer Camp**: Exit through the southern postern side-gate allows Kovur Kizhar and Ilangathir to cross into Nalankilli's encampment beyond the moat.
4. **Consistency Enforcement**: In all scenes, the river remains to the North, the fort ramparts define the inner/outer boundary, and the sunrise during the climax (Sequence 8) breaks from the East across the no-man's land.

## 3. HISTORICALLY ACCURATE SHRINE SPECIFICATION (*KOTTAM*)

### Visual Guidelines for Early Tamil Sacred Space
- **Footprint**: Modest human-scale footprint (6m x 5m rectangular courtyard with a 3m x 2.5m inner brick altar room).
- **Materials**: Fired terracotta brick foundation, dark teak/jackfruit timber support beams, and lime-wash plaster with subtle natural red-ochre border trims.
- **Lighting**: Three small terracotta oil lamps (*agal vilakku*) set in low wall niches burning sesame oil.
- **Offerings**: Fresh unthreaded white flower blossoms (including Aathipoo petals) placed gently upon a raised granite threshold slab.
- **Atmospheric Quality**: Quiet, contemplative, integrated into everyday neighborhood life.

### Narrative Interaction (Sequence 5: 480s – 520s)
- **Action**: Arumbu carries her banana-leaf wrapped food parcel through the quiet streets as dusk falls.
- **Human Gesture**: Pausing outside the small shrine niche, she sets down a handful of white petals and whispers:
  > *“எல்லோரும் நலமாகத் திரும்ப வேண்டும்.”*
  *(May everyone return safe and sound.)*
- **Thematic Integrity**: Strictly non-miraculous. The shrine is a vessel for maternal grief and hope, not an intervention point for supernatural weapons or divine rescue.
